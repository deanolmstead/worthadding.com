Head-to-head laptop power bank comparison

Anker 737 vs UGREEN Nexode 25000mAh 145W

These are close three-port USB-C power banks for travelers who need to recharge a laptop plus smaller devices away from an outlet. Anker’s A1289 is the 24,000mAh, 140W option with a color display and stated 632g weight. UGREEN’s 90597A moves to 25,000mAh and 145W total output.

Verdict

Choose UGREEN for the capacity-first default.

UGREEN Nexode 25000mAh 145W is the cleaner default for most shoppers choosing these exact models. Its official US product page lists 25,000mAh, 145W fast charging, three ports, a digital display, and a 100W USB-C cable in the box. That makes it the stronger fit when the goal is to carry one large battery for a laptop, phone, and accessories.

Anker 737 Power Bank (A1289) is the better choice if you value its maker-listed 140W USB-C output, PIQ 3.0 support, trickle charging for low-powered accessories, color display, and stated 155.8 × 54.6 × 49.65mm size. Anker also publishes a 632g weight and 24-month warranty.

Confirm the USB-C charging profile your computer accepts and use a properly rated cable before checkout.

How we compared

Read output claims in context.

Capacity is only one part of travel usefulness. UGREEN’s extra 1,000mAh is a real published difference, but it is a small nominal gap. A power bank loses energy while converting its internal battery voltage to USB output, and a laptop can continue consuming power while it charges. Treat either figure as a comparison number rather than a guaranteed recharge count.

Port labels and total output are not interchangeable. Anker identifies up to 140W through USB-C and 18W through USB-A. UGREEN advertises 145W total output. Those statements do not establish the same per-port split under every multi-device combination. Check the current manual and your laptop’s stated USB-C requirements before building a three-device setup around either unit.

FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Which high-output power bank is better for most laptop travelers?

UGREEN is the capacity-first default with its listed 25,000mAh and 145W total output. Choose Anker for its 140W USB-C capability, trickle charging mode, display, and published compact dimensions.

Can both power banks charge a laptop?

Both are positioned for USB-C laptop charging. Confirm your laptop USB-C power requirements and use an appropriately rated cable.

Which one has more battery capacity?

UGREEN lists 25,000mAh and Anker lists 24,000mAh. Usable energy varies with device draw and voltage conversion.
