Buyer guide

Choose by the lid you want to handle.

Electric can openers look simple, but the real choice is cut style, cleanup, height, counter footprint, and whether you often open pop-top cans for people who should not have to fight sharp lids.

Hamilton Beach Smooth Touch

Buy this if / skip this if

Buy this if
  • You want smooth, touchable lid edges instead of a conventional sharp top-cut lid.
  • You open pop-top cans often and would rather use a lever than pull rings by hand.
  • You have room for a taller countertop appliance and want an easy-touch, one-hand workflow.
Skip this if
  • You want the smallest possible countertop opener for a tight cabinet or apartment kitchen.
  • You strongly prefer a removable activation lever for cleaning around the blade area.
  • You only open cans occasionally and do not care enough about smooth-edge lids to give up counter height.
Cuisinart Deluxe

Smooth-edge cutting changes cleanup

A side-cut opener removes the lid around the can wall instead of punching down through the top. That matters when you drain cans, hand lids to kids, or reuse a lid briefly in the refrigerator.

Removable parts change maintenance

Cuisinart's detachable activation lever is useful if you want to clean the lid-contact area more directly. Hamilton Beach wins the lid-edge decision, but Cuisinart has the clearer cleaning convenience.

Height matters more than weight

Can openers often live under upper cabinets. Cuisinart publishes a 9.35 inch height; Hamilton Beach is positioned as extra tall, so measure the spot where the opener will actually sit.

Before you buy

Check the can, the counter, and the cleanup.

  • Measure under-cabinet height before choosing a taller smooth-edge opener.
  • If you open a lot of ring-pull cans, prioritize a model that explicitly supports pop-top cans.
  • If you hate cleaning around appliance blades, look for removable lever or cutting-assembly guidance before checkout.
  • Do not expect any countertop electric opener to handle damaged rims perfectly; dented cans can still need a manual backup.

FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Which electric can opener is better for most kitchens?

Hamilton Beach Smooth Touch is the better default because it cuts along the side of the can, leaves a smooth edge, handles pop-top and regular cans, and uses a light-touch lever that is friendly for daily use.

Why would I buy the Cuisinart Deluxe instead?

Choose Cuisinart Deluxe if you want a more compact countertop opener with a removable activation lever, Power Cut blade, magnetic lid holder, sturdy base, and three-year limited warranty.

Does the Hamilton Beach leave a smooth lid?

Yes. The Smooth Touch design cuts along the side of the can so the lid edge is smooth enough to handle and can be reused as a temporary cover.

Which one is easier to clean?

Cuisinart has the clearer cleanup advantage because its activation lever detaches for cleaning. Hamilton Beach Smooth Touch should be wiped clean and does not have the same removable cutting assembly advantage.

Should I choose by size alone?

No. Counter footprint matters, but the bigger difference is cut style: Hamilton Beach prioritizes smooth-edge lids and pop-top cans, while Cuisinart prioritizes a compact classic electric-opener setup.
