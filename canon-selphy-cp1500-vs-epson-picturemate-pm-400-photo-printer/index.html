Buyer guide

Choose by print routine.

Both printers target casual photo sharing rather than office documents. The decision comes down to whether you want a smaller dye-sub printer that can move around, or a compact home printer with 5x7 output and a more generous paper tray workflow.

Canon SELPHY CP1500

Buy this if / skip this if

Buy this if
  • You want the smaller printer body and lighter carry weight for events, craft tables, dorm rooms, or occasional travel.
  • You plan to print from several sources: phone app, AirPrint, USB-C computer or flash-drive connection, and SD-family memory cards.
  • You care about small-format output such as card prints, square labels, or 4x6 photo handouts more than 5x7 enlargements.
Skip this if
  • You want 5x7 prints from this dedicated photo printer; Canon's listed CP1500 sizes top out at postcard 4.0 x 6.0.
  • You want a larger paper handling routine for batches; Canon lists output tray capacity up to 18 sheets.
  • You prefer buying from a manufacturer-direct page that currently presents the printer as actively available.
Epson PictureMate PM-400

Buy this if / skip this if

Buy this if
  • You want one compact printer for both 4x6 snapshots and 5x7 gifts, keepsakes, or small frames.
  • You like Epson's print-pack approach because ink and 4x6 photo paper can be planned together.
  • You will mostly print at home near an outlet and value the faster listed 4x6 speed over bag-friendly size.
Skip this if
  • You need the smallest possible printer for a cabinet, event kit, or travel bag.
  • You want optional battery printing; Epson's PM-400 workflow is a plug-in compact printer setup.
  • You want Canon's card-size and square-label print formats rather than Epson's 3.5x5, 4x6, and 5x7 range.
Print size changes the whole use case

4x6 is enough for fridge photos, albums, thank-you notes, and party handouts. 5x7 support makes the Epson better for small frames, gifts, and school or family display prints without moving to a larger printer.

Portable and compact are different

The Canon stores much smaller and weighs about half as much as the Epson. The Epson is still compact, but its printing footprint expands to 15.1 inches deep, so it fits better as a home station than a bag printer.

Consumables decide ownership friction

Canon uses SELPHY paper/cassette and dye-sub cartridge planning, while Epson promotes print packs with ink and 4x6 paper together. Before buying either, check that the paper size and refill kit you expect are easy for you to reorder.

Before you buy

Check these compact photo printer details first.

  • Confirm the exact paper and cartridge kit for the printer. The printer is only half the purchase if the refill format is hard to find later.
  • Decide whether 5x7 matters. If every print will be 4x6 or smaller, Canon's smaller body may matter more than Epson's larger format support.
  • Check your source devices. Memory cards, phone apps, AirPrint, USB flash drives, and computer USB connections are not interchangeable conveniences.
  • Verify seller and listing details before checkout, especially for older or marketplace-sold models and bundled printer-plus-paper packages.

FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Which compact photo printer should most people buy?

Epson PictureMate PM-400 is the safer default if you want a compact home photo printer with 4x6 and 5x7 output, a 2.7-inch display, Wi-Fi Direct, memory card support, and a print-pack workflow built around ink plus paper.

Why choose Canon SELPHY CP1500 instead?

Choose Canon SELPHY CP1500 if portability, smaller storage size, USB-C data connection, optional battery support, a larger 3.5-inch screen, and smaller card or square label formats matter more than 5x7 output.

Do both printers make 4x6 photos?

Yes. Canon lists postcard-size 4.0 x 6.0 output for the SELPHY CP1500, while Epson lists 4 x 6 borderless photo printing for the PictureMate PM-400.

Which one is better for party or travel printing?

Canon SELPHY CP1500 is easier to justify for portable setups because Canon lists a smaller 7.2 x 2.3 x 5.2 inch body, about 1.9 pounds, and an optional battery pack. Epson is better when the printer can stay near an outlet and you want 5x7 support.
