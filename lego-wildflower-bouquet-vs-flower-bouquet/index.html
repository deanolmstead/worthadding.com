Verdict

Buy Wildflower Bouquet for variety and height. Buy Flower Bouquet for a classic floral look.

LEGO Botanicals Wildflower Bouquet 10313 wins for most adult builders because it gives you more pieces, 16 adjustable stems, eight named wildflower species, and a taller guide stem. It feels more like a loose florist arrangement and has more room for asymmetry, color variation, and taller vase styling.

LEGO Flower Bouquet 10280 is still the better fit if you want the original botanical bouquet with familiar flowers. LEGO lists roses, snapdragons, poppies, asters, daisies, and grasses, plus 15 stems that can be rearranged and resized for a display that looks tidier and more traditional.

The two sets

Both are vase-ready adult builds, but they style differently.

This comparison treats them as decorative adult LEGO bouquet sets, not children's toys or collectible investments. The practical decision is display shape, flower mix, stem count, build density, and whether you want the wilder arrangement or the cleaner original bouquet.

Buyer guide

Choose by the display you want after the build.

The box count matters, but bouquet sets live or fail by how they look in a real vase. Decide first whether you want a taller, meadow-like arrangement or a neater mixed-flower centerpiece.

Buy Wildflower Bouquet if...

  • You want the taller statement piece; LEGO lists the straight larkspur stem at over 18 inches.
  • You like identifying different blooms while building, including cornflowers, lavender, poppies, cow parsley, ferns, gerberas, larkspur, and lupins.
  • You want the fuller set on paper: 939 pieces and 16 individual adjustable stems.
Skip it if...
  • You prefer a tighter, more symmetrical table bouquet.
  • Your vase is short or narrow enough that taller stems will look crowded.
  • You want the most recognizable rose-and-daisy style instead of a field-flower mix.

Buy Flower Bouquet if...

  • You want classic flowers: LEGO names roses, snapdragons, poppies, asters, daisies, and grasses.
  • You prefer a calmer display that can sit on a desk, shelf, dining table, or gift table without needing a tall vase.
  • You like the original LEGO botanical bouquet concept and want adjustable petals, leaves, and stem lengths.
Skip it if...
  • You want the larger piece count and taller guide stem of Wildflower Bouquet.
  • You are trying to create a big combined display from one box.
  • You want a wilder, more colorful mix rather than a traditional florist-inspired arrangement.

What the specs mean

  • Stem count changes vase fullness. Sixteen stems on Wildflower Bouquet give you slightly more spread and layering than the 15-stem Flower Bouquet.
  • Guide height changes where it fits. The 18-inch larkspur guide height pushes Wildflower toward taller vases and open shelves; the 14-inch snapdragon guide is easier to place lower.
  • Piece count changes build time and texture. Wildflower's 939 pieces give it a denser build than the 756-piece Flower Bouquet, which can be a plus if the building session is part of the gift.

Before you buy

  • Neither set includes a vase, so factor in the container before gifting.
  • Both are 18+ display builds with small pieces; they are not suited to homes where loose parts will be a problem.
  • Final height depends on how you arrange the adjustable stems, not just the tallest listed flower.
  • If you plan to combine both sets, choose a wider vase or split them into two arrangements.

FAQ

Quick answers before choosing.

Which LEGO bouquet is better for a taller vase?

Wildflower Bouquet. LEGO lists the straight larkspur stem at over 18 inches tall, while the Flower Bouquet snapdragon guide height is over 14 inches.

Does either LEGO bouquet include a vase?

No. LEGO notes that a vase is not included with both sets, so the finished display needs a separate vase or container.

Which set has more stems?

Wildflower Bouquet has 16 adjustable individual stems. Flower Bouquet has 15 stems with mixed flower and leaf varieties.

Can I combine the two bouquet sets?

Yes. LEGO says Wildflower Bouquet can be combined with Flower Bouquet 10280 to make one large bouquet or two different bouquets.

Bottom line

Wildflower Bouquet is the better one-box display.

If you are buying one adult LEGO bouquet as a gift or room accent, Wildflower Bouquet gives you the bigger, taller, more varied arrangement. Choose Flower Bouquet when the classic mixed-flower look matters more than height or piece count.
