Questions we hear every day

If you are not sure where to start, these are the conversations our team has most often at the front counter.

Can you help me pick for my exact yard?

Yes. Bring photos, measurements, and a note about sun exposure. We will steer you toward practical options.

Do you stock native plants?

We keep a strong native and climate-appropriate selection, especially for pollinator and low-water gardens.

Can I reserve plants before coming in?

For many items, yes. Call ahead or email a short wish list and we will confirm what is available that week.
