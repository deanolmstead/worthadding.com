The decision is really about handling versus control.

The Worx WG163 and BLACK+DECKER LSTE525 share the core basics: 20V battery power, 12-inch cutting paths, 0.065-inch line, push-button line feeding, wheeled edging, and two included batteries. That overlap makes the details matter. The BLACK+DECKER LSTE525 is still a strong alternative. Its 2-speed control and PowerDrive transmission make it the better choice if you want a high-power setting for tougher grass and a lower-power setting to stretch runtime, especially if you already own BLACK+DECKER 20V MAX batteries.

FAQ

Questions before checkout

Are these good replacements for a gas trimmer?

They are better fits for routine trimming, edging, and light cleanup than for heavy brush or all-day commercial work. Both use 0.065-inch line and 12-inch cutting paths, which points toward homeowner maintenance rather than clearing thick growth.

Which one is lighter to handle?

The Worx WG163 is the lighter published machine-weight pick at 5.29 pounds. BLACK+DECKER publishes an 8.8-pound weight on its LSTE525 product page, so shoppers who prioritize less arm fatigue should start with the Worx.

Do both include batteries and a charger?

Yes. The Worx WG163 kit includes two 20V PowerShare batteries and a charger, and the BLACK+DECKER LSTE525 kit includes two 20V MAX lithium-ion batteries and a charger.

Which is better if I already own batteries?

Buy inside the battery system you already own unless the tool itself solves a specific problem. Worx makes the most sense for Worx PowerShare owners; BLACK+DECKER makes the most sense for 20V MAX owners.

Why is the BLACK+DECKER still worth considering?

It adds 2-speed control and PowerDrive transmission, so it is the more compelling choice when you want a high-power setting for tougher patches and a lower-speed setting to stretch runtime.

Best overall

Worx WG163

Start with the Worx if your yard work is mostly weekly trimming, fence-line cleanup, and sidewalk edging. It gives you the shared 20V, 12-inch basics with the more comfort-forward spec sheet.
