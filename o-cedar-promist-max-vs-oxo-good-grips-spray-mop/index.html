Buyer guide

Choose by cleaning routine.

Both mops are reusable-pad, refillable-bottle alternatives to disposable sheet systems. The decision is whether you want O-Cedar's longer continuous pad workflow or OXO's integrated scrubber.

O-Cedar ProMist MAX

Buy this if / skip this if

Buy this if
  • You want one washable pad that can clean longer before a swap because the mop head flips to a second side.
  • You prefer a no-battery trigger and refillable bottle instead of proprietary liquid cartridges.
  • Your routine is quick maintenance cleaning across several sealed hard-floor rooms.
Skip this if
  • You often deal with dried-on kitchen spots and want a scrubber built directly into the mop head.
  • You want official published dimensions before buying; O-Cedar's current page emphasizes features rather than size specs.
  • You would rather use a bucket-and-mop system for heavier floor washing sessions.
OXO Good Grips

Buy this if / skip this if

Buy this if
  • You want a slide-out scrubber for dried drips, sticky spots, and small floor marks.
  • You like OXO's published 56.5-inch overall height and want dimensions before purchase.
  • You want a pivoting microfiber head with available scrubber refills.
Skip this if
  • You would rather flip a double-sided pad and keep mopping than deploy a separate scrubber piece.
  • You want the strongest official refill story around a dedicated spray-mop pad replacement.
  • You mainly need light daily passes and do not expect the scrubber to matter.
A two-sided pad changes pace

O-Cedar's flip head lets you clean with one pad side, turn the head over, and keep moving. That matters more for broad maintenance cleaning than for tiny spot jobs.

A scrubber changes spot cleanup

OXO's slide-out scrubber is the better feature when the mess is stuck to the floor. It reduces the chance that you will need to stop and find a separate sponge or brush.

Refill bottles beat cartridge lock-in

Both mops use refillable bottles, so the ongoing routine is less tied to one cleaning-liquid cartridge. Confirm the cleaning solution matches your floor manufacturer's care instructions.

Before you buy

Check these spray mop details first.

  • Confirm your flooring can use the solution you plan to put in the bottle; sealed wood, laminate, stone, and tile can have different care rules.
  • Check replacement pad availability before choosing a mop, especially if you want separate clean pads for kitchen, bathroom, and entryway work.
  • Decide whether you clean wide areas or stuck spots more often. That one habit determines whether O-Cedar's flip head or OXO's scrubber is more useful.
  • Plan where the mop will hang or stand. Spray mops are tall, and the bottle/head shape can matter in a narrow pantry or utility closet.
