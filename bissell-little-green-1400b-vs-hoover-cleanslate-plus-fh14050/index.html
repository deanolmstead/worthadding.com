Fit check

Choose by the mess you actually clean.

Both machines are useful because they can live close to the problem. The better buy depends on whether your normal mess is an occasional spot or a repeating pet-and-upholstery cleanup loop.

Buy BISSELL Little Green if

You want the safer general-purpose recommendation: a 48-ounce clean tank, carry handle, 15-foot cord, and compact tool set for coffee spills, sofa marks, stairs, rugs, and occasional car detailing. It is the better fit when you do not need a wide cleaning path every week.

Skip it if your priority is a broader attachment for large stairs or a published dirty-water capacity. Hoover gives you more detail on those points.

Buy Hoover CleanSlate Plus if

You regularly deal with pet accidents, muddy paw prints, stairs, or larger spot areas and want the 7-inch WidePath Tool plus a TightSpot Tool. Hoover also publishes a 40-ounce clean tank, 23-ounce dirty tank, and 4.5-foot hose for the CleanSlate Pet line.

Skip it if you want the least model ambiguity. The official Hoover page reviewed is for FH14000V, while the selected Amazon listing is FH14050, so confirm the exact bundle and model identifier at checkout.

Quick fit check

Neither machine is a replacement for a full-size carpet extractor when you need to clean an entire home. These are spot-cleaning tools: useful because they are smaller, faster to grab, and better matched to isolated messes. Follow surface-care labels, test a hidden area, and use only compatible solution directions.

FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Which portable carpet cleaner is better for most people?

The BISSELL Little Green 1400B is the better default for most people because its published combination of a 48-ounce clean tank, 9.65-pound weight, 15-foot cord, carry handle, and familiar three-tool setup makes it a straightforward all-purpose spot cleaner. Choose the Hoover CleanSlate Plus when its wider 7-inch tool and pet-mess-oriented tool set are more important than the simpler default.

Is the Hoover CleanSlate Plus more powerful than the BISSELL Little Green?

Hoover markets the CleanSlate Pet line as 30% more powerful than the BISSELL Little Green in its own comparison material. That is a manufacturer claim, not an independent lab result in this comparison, so treat it as positioning rather than a universal performance guarantee.

Which one is better for pet stains?

Both are designed for spot cleaning pet messes, upholstery, carpet, stairs, and auto interiors. Hoover has the clearer pet-focused tool story in the official material, including a 7-inch WidePath Tool, TightSpot Tool, hose-rinse tool, and trial solution. BISSELL is the calmer pick when you want a compact general-purpose cleaner and already have a preferred formula.

How large are the clean-water tanks?

BISSELL lists a 48-ounce clean tank. Hoover lists a 40-ounce clean-water capacity and a 23-ounce dirty-water capacity. That gives BISSELL the larger stated clean-tank baseline, while Hoover publishes the more complete tank specification.

Can either machine clean a car interior or couch?

Yes. Both manufacturers position these portable cleaners for upholstery, carpet, stairs, and vehicle interiors. Always check the care label or surface manufacturer guidance, test an inconspicuous area, and follow the current machine and solution directions before cleaning a visible surface.
