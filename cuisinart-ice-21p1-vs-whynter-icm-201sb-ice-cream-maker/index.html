Buyer guide

Pick by planning style first, capacity second.

The biggest difference is not flavor support; both cover common frozen desserts. The real decision is whether you are willing to keep a bowl frozen or want a machine that can chill itself.

Cuisinart ICE-21P1

Buy this if / skip this if

Buy this if
  • You can keep the freezer bowl frozen before dessert nights.
  • You want a smaller machine for occasional 1.5-quart batches.
  • You prefer one-switch simplicity over timers and compressor settings.
Skip this if
  • You want to make multiple batches without waiting for another bowl freeze cycle.
  • Your freezer is already tight and cannot spare room for the bowl.
  • You want a machine that chills warm or recently mixed bases on its own.
Whynter ICM-201SB

Buy this if / skip this if

Buy this if
  • You want to churn without pre-freezing a canister.
  • You make frozen desserts often enough to justify a larger appliance.
  • You want the bigger 2.1-quart batch size and extended cooling features.
Skip this if
  • You only make ice cream a few times a year and want minimal storage bulk.
  • You have limited counter space for an upright compressor machine.
  • A 1.5-quart batch is already enough for your household.
Freezer bowl vs compressor

Cuisinart shifts the freezing work to your freezer before you churn. Whynter builds that chilling into the machine, which removes planning friction but makes the appliance larger.

Capacity changes the batch rhythm

The ICE-21P1 tops out at 1.5 quarts, enough for a small household batch. The ICM-201SB reaches 2.1 quarts, which is more useful for entertaining or repeat family servings.

Storage is part of ownership

A freezer-bowl model stores like a small appliance plus a bowl. A compressor model needs more shelf or counter space but does not reserve freezer room before every batch.

Before you buy

Check your freezer, counter, and batch habits.

  • Measure freezer space before choosing the Cuisinart; the bowl has to be frozen before use.
  • Measure appliance storage before choosing the Whynter; compressor machines are not occasional drawer gadgets.
  • Chill your dessert base unless the recipe says otherwise; colder starting mixes usually churn better.
  • Neither machine replaces recipe technique: fat level, sugar, stabilizers, and resting time still shape texture.

FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Which ice cream maker is better for most people?

Cuisinart ICE-21P1 is the better default for most shoppers because it is smaller, simpler, and covers the core homemade ice cream workflow as long as you can pre-freeze the bowl.

Why would I pay more for the Whynter ICM-201SB?

Choose Whynter if pre-freezing a bowl is the dealbreaker. Its built-in compressor lets the machine chill itself, supports a larger 2.1-quart batch, and is better for repeat use.

Does the Cuisinart ICE-21P1 need ice or salt?

No. Cuisinart lists a double-insulated freezer bowl, so you freeze the bowl ahead of time instead of packing ice and salt around it.

Which one is easier to store?

Cuisinart is easier to store as a smaller freezer-bowl machine, but the bowl needs freezer room before use. Whynter removes freezer-bowl planning but takes more appliance storage space.

Can both make more than ice cream?

Yes. Cuisinart lists frozen yogurt, ice cream, and sorbet. Whynter positions the ICM-201SB for ice cream, gelato, and sorbet with self-cooling operation.
