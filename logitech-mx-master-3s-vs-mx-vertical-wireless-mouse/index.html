Buyer guide

Choose by hand shape and workflow.

A mouse can have a great spec sheet and still be wrong for your hand. Decide first whether you want a traditional sculpted productivity mouse or a vertical shape, then compare features.

MX Master 3S

Buy this if / skip this if

Buy this if
  • You spend the day moving through spreadsheets, documents, timelines, browser tabs, or long pages.
  • You want Logitech's 8,000 DPI sensor and glass-tracking support for varied desk surfaces.
  • You will actually use Logi Options+ to tune buttons, scroll behavior, and app-specific shortcuts.
Skip this if
  • You are shopping specifically because you want an upright vertical mouse shape.
  • You dislike heavier sculpted mice with thumb rests and multiple controls under your hand.
  • You only need basic pointing and scrolling, not a premium workflow mouse.
MX Vertical

Buy this if / skip this if

Buy this if
  • You already know an upright handshake-style mouse feels better for your daily pointer work.
  • Your hand is in Logitech's large-to-medium fit target and you want a tall thumb-rest body.
  • You value a shape change more than the MX Master 3S sensor, scroll, and shortcut feature set.
Skip this if
  • You have never used a vertical mouse and need a safe default for shared or uncertain desk setups.
  • You want MagSpeed scrolling, very high DPI headroom, or the broad productivity-control layout.
  • Your desk tray, bag, or keyboard position does not have room for a taller mouse profile.
8K DPI is not only for huge monitors

The MX Master 3S sensor headroom lets you lower or raise sensitivity in software without fighting the surface. It is useful when you move between desk mats, bare desks, and high-resolution displays.

Scroll feel changes the day

MagSpeed matters when you live in long documents, code, calendars, or spreadsheets. The time saved is small per action, but it repeats all day.

Vertical shape is a commitment

The MX Vertical body changes your wrist and hand angle at the desk. That can be the point, but it also means it is less universal for shared desks, travel bags, and people switching from standard mice.

Before you buy

Check your desk setup before choosing a shape.

  • Measure the clearance under keyboard trays or monitor shelves if you are considering the taller MX Vertical shape.
  • Confirm Logi Options+ support for your computer if button customization is part of the purchase reason.
  • If several people share the same mouse, MX Master 3S is the safer default because its shape is less specialized.
  • If you use glass, glossy, or mixed desk surfaces, MX Master 3S has the clearer official tracking claim.

FAQ

Questions before checkout.

The right choice depends on whether you want a better all-around productivity mouse or a specifically vertical one.

Which Logitech mouse is better for most desk work?

MX Master 3S is the better default for most desk work because it has the stronger published productivity feature set: 8,000 DPI tracking, glass-surface support, Quiet Clicks, MagSpeed scrolling, and Logi Options+ customization.

Who should buy MX Vertical instead?

Buy MX Vertical if you specifically want Logitech's upright 57-degree vertical shape and your hand size matches the large-to-medium fit Logitech describes.

Do these mice use the same shape?

No. MX Master 3S is a sculpted horizontal productivity mouse with a thumb-rest workflow, while MX Vertical uses a taller handshake-style body.

Is MX Master 3S worth choosing if I use glass or mixed desk surfaces?

It is the clearer choice for that use case because Logitech specifically lists glass tracking support for MX Master 3S at a 4 mm minimum glass thickness.

Should I buy a vertical mouse without trying one?

Only if you are comfortable with a shape change. Vertical mice feel different from standard mice, so MX Master 3S is the safer default when you are buying for a shared desk or an uncertain preference.
