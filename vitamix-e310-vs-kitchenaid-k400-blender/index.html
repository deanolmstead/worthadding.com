Vitamix E310 is the better overall buy for most home cooks.

The deciding difference is what each motor is asked to do. The E310's 2 peak HP motor, hardened blades, and included mini tamper are built for the thick end of blending — nut butters, hummus, frozen desserts, and soups heated by friction — where a tamper and fine speed control matter more than a preset button. The K400's 1.5 peak HP motor is genuinely capable on drinks, but it has less headroom and no tamper in the standard box to work a thick mixture down into the blade.

On controls the split reverses. The K400 offers preset programs for Ice Crush, Icy Drinks, Smoothie, and Self-Cleaning on top of its 5-speed dial, and its Intelli-Speed control holds speed under load while Soft Start avoids the initial splatter. The E310 is manual only: 10 variable speeds and Pulse, no programs. If your blending is mostly smoothies and frozen drinks, the K400's presets are the easier daily routine.

Ownership favors the E310 on paper. Both carry 5-year coverage, but Vitamix's is a full warranty — parts, performance, labor, and two-way shipping — while the K400's jar is the bigger one (56 oz against 48 oz) and its die-cast base and shorter 15.9-inch height suit a drinks station. Buy the K400 for batch drinks and presets; buy the E310 for power, control, and the warranty behind them.

FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Answers match the FAQ structured data on this page.

Which blender is more powerful?

The Vitamix E310. It is rated at 2 peak HP and 1,440 W, against the KitchenAid K400's 1.5 peak HP and 1,200 W. Both figures are the manufacturers' own published ratings.

Which blender has the bigger jar?

The KitchenAid K400. Its standard jar holds 56 oz, against the Vitamix E310's 48 oz low-profile container. The E310's smaller jar is the trade for its 18-inch under-cabinet height and tamper-friendly thick blending.

Do either of these blenders have preset programs?

Only the KitchenAid K400. It offers Ice Crush, Icy Drinks, Smoothie, and Self-Cleaning presets alongside its 5-speed dial. The Vitamix E310 is manual only, with 10 variable speeds and a Pulse switch.

Is a tamper included?

Yes with the Vitamix E310 — a mini tamper is in the box, which is what makes its thick blends practical. The standard KitchenAid K400 box (KSB4027) includes the 56 oz jar and lid only; the tamper belongs to other K400 packages and accessories.

How do the warranties compare?

Both run 5 years, but the terms differ. The Vitamix E310's is a full warranty covering parts, performance, labor, and two-way shipping. The KitchenAid K400 carries a 5-year limited warranty.
