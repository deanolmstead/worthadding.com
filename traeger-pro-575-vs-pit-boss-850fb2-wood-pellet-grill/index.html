Head-to-head wood pellet grill comparison

Traeger Pro 575 vs Pit Boss 850FB2

Traeger Pro 575 and Pit Boss 850FB2 both solve the same backyard problem: a wood-pellet hopper grill that smokes, bakes, roasts, and grills without babysitting a fire. The real difference is control versus capacity. The Pro 575 pairs a smaller cooking area with WiFIRE app control and Alexa support, while the 850FB2 trades that smart-home layer for more grilling space, a bigger hopper, and a Flame Broiler lever for true direct-flame searing.

The Pro 575 is the better everyday buy unless you need more cooking space or direct-flame searing.

Both grills do the same core job: burn 100% hardwood pellets to smoke, bake, roast, and grill on one machine. The Traeger Pro 575 pulls ahead for most buyers because of its WiFIRE technology -- you get real-time temperature graphs, remote start and shutdown, and Alexa voice commands through the Traeger app, all backed by the D2 drivetrain that Traeger says starts quicker and holds smoke quality better than the previous generation. Porcelain-coated grates and a built-in meat probe round out a grill that is easy to set and forget for weeknight cooking.

Pit Boss 850FB2 is still the right call if capacity or searing power matters more than app control. Its 840 square inches of two-tiered cooking space beats the Pro 575's 575 square inches by a wide margin, its 21 lb hopper needs refilling less often on long smokes, and the Flame Broiler lever opens a direct path to the fire pot for searing up to 1,000°F -- something the Pro 575 cannot do at all. The trade-off is no WiFi or app control, just an LCD digital controller with 5°F increment settings.

Fit check

Choose by cook, not the sticker on the box.

The right pick depends less on the biggest cooking-area number and more on whether you want app control from the couch or a bigger grill you fully operate at the unit itself.

Buy Traeger Pro 575 if

You want to check and adjust the grill from your phone or with Alexa, cook mostly for a household of two to four, and value a quicker-starting, more consistent smoke over raw square footage.

Skip it if you regularly host large cookouts -- 575 sq in fills up fast with multiple racks of ribs or a full brisket plus sides.

Buy Pit Boss 850FB2 if

You cook for a crowd, want the option to sear steaks directly over an open flame, and are fine running the grill from its onboard LCD controller instead of a phone app.

Skip it if WiFi monitoring and Alexa control matter to you -- the 850FB2 has no app or smart-home connectivity.

FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Which grill has more cooking space?

The Pit Boss 850FB2 has 840 sq in of total cooking space across two tiers, well ahead of the Traeger Pro 575's 575 sq in (418 sq in main grate plus 154 sq in second tier). If you regularly cook multiple racks of ribs, a full brisket plus sides, or feed a crowd, the 850FB2's extra room matters more than any other spec.

Does either grill have WiFi or app control?

Only the Traeger Pro 575. Its WiFIRE technology connects the grill to home WiFi and the Traeger app, letting you monitor and adjust temperature remotely, and it also supports Alexa voice commands. The Pit Boss 850FB2 runs entirely from its onboard LCD digital controller with no WiFi, app, or Alexa connectivity.

Can either grill sear steaks with direct flame?

The Pit Boss 850FB2 can. Its Flame Broiler lever opens a direct path to the fire pot for searing up to 1,000°F. The Traeger Pro 575 is a closed-lid, indirect-heat pellet grill with a 500°F max and no direct-flame searing mode, so it relies on high indirect heat rather than open flame for a sear.

How big are the pellet hoppers?

The Traeger Pro 575 holds 18 lb of pellets and the Pit Boss 850FB2 holds 21 lb. The larger 850FB2 hopper needs refilling less often on long cooks like an overnight brisket smoke, though both need periodic topping off for all-day sessions.

Which one is easier to fit on a smaller patio?

The Traeger Pro 575 has a smaller assembled footprint at 41"L x 22"W x 53"H versus the Pit Boss 850FB2's roughly 47.2"L x 26.8"W x 47"H per Pit Boss's published spec. If patio or deck space is tight, the Pro 575's narrower stance is the easier fit.
