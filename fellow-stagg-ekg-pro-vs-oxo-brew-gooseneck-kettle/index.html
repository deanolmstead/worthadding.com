Buyer guide

Choose by workflow, not countertop style.

Both kettles are built around controlled gooseneck pouring and variable temperature water. The real decision is whether you want a simple brew timer and 30-minute hold, or a more configurable kettle with scheduling, presets, longer hold choices, and app-linked updates.

Fellow Stagg EKG Pro

Buy this if / skip this if

Buy this if
  • You want the kettle ready on a schedule or you make coffee at a fixed time most mornings.
  • You share the kettle with tea drinkers or multiple coffee recipes and want Guide Mode presets plus finer hold-time choices.
  • You care about setup details such as altitude, chime volume, firmware updates, and a color screen with more visible status.

  • You only need one target temperature and a timer for a single daily pour-over routine.

Hold time

OXO's 30-minute hold is enough for most single-session brewing. Fellow's 15-, 30-, 45-, and 60-minute choices matter if you linger over multiple pours or share water across coffee and tea.

Temperature range

OXO publishes a 104 to 212°F range. Fellow emphasizes to-the-degree Fahrenheit control and to-the-half-degree Celsius control, which matters most if you regularly compare recipes by small changes.

Controls

OXO is intentionally direct: twist the dial, heat, time the brew. Fellow gives you more settings to configure, which is helpful only if you will actually use scheduling, presets, and altitude adjustment.

Before you buy

Check these kettle fit points first.

  • Confirm capacity against your actual brew size. Gooseneck kettles are often smaller than general-purpose electric kettles.
  • Think about speed expectations. Gooseneck spouts are built for control, not dumping a lot of water quickly into a teapot.
  • Plan for descaling if you have hard water; mineral buildup slows heating and can shorten the useful life of any electric kettle.
  • At higher elevations, boiling behavior changes. Follow the maker's altitude and max-fill guidance instead of forcing 212°F.

FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Which gooseneck kettle is better for most people?

The OXO Brew Adjustable Temperature Pour-Over Kettle is the better default pick for most pour-over drinkers because it covers the core workflow with 104 to 212°F temperature selection, a 30-minute keep-warm mode, a built-in count-up timer, a 360-degree base, and a simple one-dial interface.

Why would someone choose the Fellow Stagg EKG Pro?

Choose the Fellow Stagg EKG Pro if you want advanced controls such as scheduling, adjustable 15-, 30-, 45-, or 60-minute hold times, altitude adjustment, Guide Mode presets, Wi-Fi firmware updates through the Fellow Updater app, and a color screen.

Do both kettles have a built-in brew timer?

Yes. OXO lists an integrated count-up timer for pour-over tracking, while Fellow lists a Brew Stopwatch after the water is heated.

Which kettle has more hold-mode flexibility?

Fellow has the more flexible hold settings because Fellow lists adjustable 15-, 30-, 45-, and 60-minute hold options. OXO holds the selected temperature for 30 minutes.

Should you use these for tea as well as coffee?

Both can heat water across common coffee and tea temperature ranges, but their gooseneck spouts are most useful for controlled pour-over coffee. If you mainly fill teapots quickly, a larger non-gooseneck kettle may fit better.
