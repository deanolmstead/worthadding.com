Fit check

Choose by routine, not salad-spinner snobbery.

Both are manual spinners with a bowl, basket, lid, stop control, and non-slip base. Choose by batch size, spin control, and storage shape.

Buy the OXO if

You wash a full head of lettuce, several servings of greens, herbs, or produce in one go. OXO gives separate outer-bowl and inner-basket capacities; its 4.95-quart basket is the relevant number for produce, while the 6.22-quart bowl gives it room to spin.

The soft non-slip knob supports top-down spinning, the brake stops the basket, the basket doubles as a colander, and the lid comes apart for cleaning. Skip it if a 10.5-inch round footprint is awkward where you prep or store salad tools.

Buy the Cuisinart if

Your counter or cabinet is shallow enough that the difference between an 8.2-inch and 10.5-inch width matters. Cuisinart lists an 8.3 by 8.2 by 8.2 inch body, locking tabs, nonskid base, and clear serving base; the selected Amazon listing identifies a 5-quart model.

Cuisinart says you can add and drain water through the top opening without removing the lid, turn the spin knob, and press the brake to stop. Skip it if you prefer a low, one-handed pump setup or need a published basket-capacity figure.

Quick fit check: Measure the storage space and picture your usual greens. OXO is 10.5 inches wide but 6 inches tall; Cuisinart is roughly 8.2 inches wide and 8.2 inches tall.

FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Which salad spinner is better for most people?

The OXO Good Grips Salad Spinner, SKU 32480, is the better default for most people. OXO lists a 6.22-quart bowl and 4.95-quart basket in a 10.5 by 10.5 by 6 inch body, with one-handed pump spinning, a brake, a non-slip base, a colander basket, and a lid that comes apart for cleaning. Choose the Cuisinart CTG-00-SAS1 when its smaller 8.3 by 8.2 by 8.2 inch footprint, hand-crank control, top opening for adding and draining water, locking tabs, or lifetime warranty better fits your kitchen.

Is the OXO spinner bigger than the Cuisinart?

Yes in the published measurements. OXO lists a 6.22-quart bowl and 4.95-quart basket, plus a 10.5 by 10.5 by 6 inch overall size. The selected Cuisinart Amazon listing identifies a 5-quart model, and Cuisinart lists its CTG-00-SAS1 at 8.3 by 8.2 by 8.2 inches. Capacity labels are not necessarily measured the same way across brands, so use the dimensions as well as the quart label when deciding where it will live.

What is the practical difference between pump and hand-crank spinners?

The OXO uses a top pump and brake button, while Cuisinart describes an easy-to-turn spin knob and brake button. The OXO format suits shoppers who want one-handed downward pumping; the Cuisinart format suits shoppers who prefer turning a handle. Neither control style is automatically better at every task, so choose the motion you will be happiest using and cleaning after frequent prep.

Can the bowls be used for serving?

Both brands describe a second role for the outer bowl. OXO says the basket doubles as a colander, while Cuisinart says its clear base can be used as a serving bowl. These are useful workflow details, but confirm the current listing’s included pieces and care instructions before ordering.

How should I compare cleaning features?

Start with the parts you will handle. OXO says its lid comes apart for easy cleaning and marks the spinner dishwasher safe. Cuisinart lists dishwasher-safe care and emphasizes its top opening for adding and draining water without removing the lid. This comparison does not make a durability or performance guarantee; use each manufacturer’s current instructions for cleaning and reassembly.
