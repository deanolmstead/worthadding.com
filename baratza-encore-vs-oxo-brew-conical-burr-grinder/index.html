Buyer guide

Choose by routine, not coffee snobbery.

Both grinders are sensible upgrades from a blade grinder for brewed coffee. The real decision is whether you want more manual control over grind size or a more automatic daily workflow for a household coffee maker.

Baratza Encore

Buy this if / skip this if

Buy this if
  • You switch between pour-over, drip, French press, AeroPress, or cold brew and want 40 stepped settings to correct grind size without jumping too far.
  • Your counter space is narrow; the Encore is a tall, compact grinder with a smaller listed footprint than the OXO.
  • You care about long-term serviceability and like that Baratza publishes replacement parts and support around its grinder platform.
Skip this if
  • You want the grinder to remember a timed dose so anyone in the house can repeat the same batch with one button.
  • You keep a large amount of beans in the hopper and would rather have OXO's larger listed 12 oz bean capacity.
  • You mostly brew one automatic drip recipe and do not want to think about grind time or bean dose each morning.
OXO Brew Conical Burr

Buy this if / skip this if

Buy this if
  • You make drip coffee most mornings and want the timer to repeat the previous grind time after you choose your setting.
  • You prefer a larger listed hopper and a removable grounds container sized for bigger household batches.
  • You want a straightforward countertop grinder for automatic drip, French press, and cold brew without managing a more granular setting map.
Skip this if
  • You enjoy dialing pour-over recipes and want smaller adjustment jumps than the OXO's 15 main settings provide.
  • You have a shallow counter or shelf; OXO's listed 11.8 inch depth needs more front-to-back room.
  • You want a grinder known for parts-forward long-term ownership rather than a simpler sealed-appliance feel.
Grind steps

More settings do not automatically make better coffee, but they make correction easier. If a pour-over drains too slowly, the Encore gives you more intermediate places to move before the cup swings too far the other way.

Hopper capacity

OXO's larger hopper is convenient for households that leave beans loaded. Baratza's smaller 300 g hopper is still enough for multiple pots, but it nudges you toward loading fresher amounts more often.

Counter footprint

The Encore is narrower and shallower on paper, which helps beside a kettle or brewer. The OXO is deeper and taller, so measure under cabinets before assuming it fits your coffee bar.

Before you buy

Check these grinder-specific fit points first.

  • Do not buy either grinder for demanding espresso dialing; this page is about brewed coffee routines, not a dedicated espresso grinder setup.
  • Measure counter depth with the hopper and grounds bin access in mind, not just the listed footprint.
  • Decide whether you single-dose beans or leave them in the hopper. Single-dosing favors manual control; hopper storage favors OXO's timer routine.
  • Plan for cleaning. Burr grinders hold coffee oils and fines, so either pick needs regular brushing and occasional burr-area maintenance.

FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Which grinder is better for pour-over coffee?

The Baratza Encore is the better choice for pour-over because its 40 stepped settings give you more room to adjust drawdown and flavor than the OXO's 15 main settings.

Which grinder is easier for automatic drip coffee?

The OXO Brew Conical Burr is easier if your routine is the same drip batch every morning. Its timer remembers the previous grind time, so repeating a batch takes less attention.

Do either of these replace a dedicated espresso grinder?

No. Both can grind finely, but this comparison is aimed at brewed coffee. If espresso is the main goal, shop for a grinder built around espresso adjustment and portafilter workflow.

Which grinder has the smaller counter footprint?

The Baratza Encore is smaller on paper at 13 x 15 x 34 cm, about 5.1 x 5.9 x 13.4 inches. OXO lists the Brew Conical Burr at 6.8 x 11.8 x 14.8 inches.

Which one should a beginner buy?

Buy the Baratza Encore if you want to learn how grind size changes coffee. Buy the OXO if you mostly want a simple timed grinder for a drip machine.
