FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Which bag is better for most frequent travelers?

The Travelpro Platinum Elite is the better default because it is meaningfully lighter, still spacious at 46 L, and includes the travel-day features most people actually use: front pockets, suiter storage, spinner wheels, expansion, and USB pass-through.

When is the Briggs & Riley worth paying more for?

Choose the Briggs & Riley if you travel often enough that lifetime repair coverage, the flat-packing Outsider handle, CX compression, and the integrated garment carrier matter more than carrying a lighter empty bag.

Do either of these fit every airline carry-on sizer?

No carry-on is universal. The Travelpro lists a 23.5 x 14.5 x 9 in overall size, while the Briggs & Riley lists 22 x 14 x 9-11.5 in depending on expansion. Check your airline rules before flying, especially when either bag is expanded.

Which one is better for suits or dress clothes?

The Briggs & Riley has the edge for formalwear because its Outsider handle keeps the packing surface flat and its integrated garment carrier is central to the design. The Travelpro still includes a drop-in fold-out suiter and is good for ordinary business trips.

Should I choose softside luggage over hardside luggage?

Softside luggage is usually better when you want exterior pockets, flexible packing, and quick access during travel. Hardside luggage is better if shell structure and wipe-clean protection matter more than front-pocket convenience.
