Buyer guide

Choose by the backup job.

A 1kWh station can be great for laptops, router/modem gear, lights, CPAP-style loads after checking requirements, mini-fridges, camping devices, and short outage coverage. It is not a whole-home battery, and the right choice depends on load size, recharge plan, solar setup, and whether you will expand later.

Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2

Buy this if / skip this if

Buy this if
  • You want the stronger rated inverter in this head-to-head for tools, kitchen appliances, or mixed outage loads that may exceed 1,800W.
  • You value a lighter 24.9 lb body because the station will move between garage, car, campsite, and house.
  • You want a fast wall-recharge routine and are comfortable enabling UltraFast charging in the Anker app when speed matters.
Skip this if
  • You already own EcoFlow batteries or plan to build a larger modular DELTA system.
  • You need the highest published solar-input ceiling in this matchup for a semi-fixed off-grid setup.
  • You want computer/NAS UPS software integration more than simple standby backup behavior.
EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus

  • You want EcoFlow's Power Manager/NAS-oriented UPS workflow for a desk, network closet, or small server setup.
  • Your solar plan can actually use EcoFlow's higher published total solar input with compatible panels and correct voltage limits.
Skip this if
  • You want the highest rated AC output in the smallest self-contained 1kWh box here.
  • You will carry the station often and care about every few pounds.
  • You are not buying extra batteries, alternator charging gear, or solar panels, because EcoFlow's ecosystem advantage will matter less.
1,024Wh is not 1,024Wh at the outlet

Inverter losses and reserve behavior reduce usable AC energy. A 100W device will not reliably run for 10.2 hours just because the battery label says 1,024Wh.

Rated output changes appliance fit

Anker's 2,000W rating gives more room for brief kitchen, tool, or appliance loads. EcoFlow's 1,800W rating is still substantial, but load math matters before plugging in heaters, kettles, or compressors.

Solar wattage only helps in the right setup

EcoFlow publishes a higher total solar-input ceiling, while Anker lists 600W max. Either way, panel angle, weather, cable compatibility, voltage limits, and real sun exposure decide whether that number matters.

Before you buy

Map the load before picking the brand.

  • Add the running watts and startup surge for the devices you actually need during an outage; space heaters and electric cooking gear drain 1kWh stations quickly.
  • For refrigerators, medicine storage, CPAP machines, routers, and NAS gear, test your exact equipment before relying on any portable station overnight.
  • Check the solar-panel voltage range and connector requirements before assuming panels from another kit will work safely.
  • Do not store a power station empty in a closet for storm season. Follow the maker's storage and maintenance guidance so the battery is ready when needed.

FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Which portable power station is better for most people?

Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2 is the better default for most 1kWh shoppers. It has the same listed capacity as DELTA 3 Plus, higher rated AC output, faster published full recharge, a lighter body, and a simpler one-box value case.

Why choose EcoFlow DELTA 3 Plus instead?

Choose EcoFlow if you want expandable capacity, EcoFlow extra-battery compatibility, higher published total solar input, or the PC/NAS-oriented Power Manager workflow. It is the better platform pick, even though Anker is the stronger standalone box.

Can either unit run a whole house?

No. These are portable 1,024Wh stations for selected loads, not whole-home batteries. They can be useful for routers, laptops, lights, camping devices, and short appliance backup, but high-draw heating and cooking loads drain this class quickly.

Are these safe replacements for a medical UPS?

Treat them as standby backup tools, not medical-grade life-support power. Check your device maker's wattage, surge behavior, runtime needs, and backup recommendations before relying on any portable station for medical equipment.

Should solar input decide the purchase?

Only if you will buy compatible panels and use them in strong sun. EcoFlow publishes higher total solar input, while Anker's 600W ceiling is still useful for a portable setup. Panel voltage, weather, cable fit, and placement matter as much as the headline wattage.
