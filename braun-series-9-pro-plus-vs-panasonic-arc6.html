FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Which one is better for sensitive skin?

Start with the Braun if skin comfort is your first concern. Its recommendation here comes from the comfort-oriented system: 5 synchronized elements, ProComfort prep attachment, AutoSense, sonic glide, and a 40-degree adaptive head.

Which one is better for thick or dense stubble?

The Panasonic ARC6 is the more aggressive spec-sheet choice for dense stubble. It uses 6 blades, a linear motor, 84,000 cross-cutting actions per minute, a 22D flexible head, and an intelligent beard sensor.

Do both work wet and dry?

Yes. Braun describes the Series 9 PRO+ as 100% waterproof for wet or dry use. Panasonic lists the ES-LS9A-K as waterproof, wet/dry, and washable.

Do both include cleaning stations?

Yes. The Braun 9597cc includes a 6-in-1 SmartCare Center. The Panasonic ES-LS9A-K is the ARC6 variant with a clean and charge station.

How often will I need replacement blades or foils?

Braun recommends replacing the shaver head every 18 months for optimal performance. Panasonic lists the WES9600 replacement blade and foil set for ARC6 shavers and says replacing blades and foil every 1 to 2 years helps keep the shave smooth and comfortable.
