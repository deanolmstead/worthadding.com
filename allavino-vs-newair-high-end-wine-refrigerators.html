FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Which wine refrigerator holds more bottles?

Allavino publishes a 172-bottle capacity for the VSWR172-2SR20. Newair publishes a 160-bottle capacity for the AWR-1600DB. In either case, real capacity can drop with wider Burgundy, Champagne, or irregular bottle shapes.

Can both be built into cabinetry?

Yes. Allavino describes the FlexCount II cabinet as usable freestanding or built into cabinetry, and Newair lists the AWR-1600DB as built-in or freestanding. Leave the manufacturer-required clearances and confirm the hinge swing before ordering.

Which one is better for mixed red and white storage?

Both are dual-zone models. Newair publishes 40-50 F for the upper zone and 50-66 F for the lower zone. Allavino publishes independent dual-zone controls and a 40-56 F upper zone / 54-65 F lower zone range for this model family, which also covers typical white and red storage targets.

Why is Allavino the winner if Newair is simpler?

At this tier, bottle fit and temperature stability matter more than convenience alone. Allavino's FlexCount shelving and Tru-Vino control system make it the stronger long-term collection pick, while Newair is still a good alternative for buyers who value a straightforward large cabinet.

Should I expect either published capacity to match my real collection?

Not exactly. Wine-fridge capacities are usually based on standard Bordeaux-style bottles. If your collection includes wider Pinot Noir, Champagne, magnums, or unusually tall bottles, plan for fewer bottles than the headline number.
