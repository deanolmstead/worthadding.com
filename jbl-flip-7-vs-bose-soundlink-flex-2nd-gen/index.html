Buyer guide

Choose by use case, not brand loyalty.

Both are compact enough for everyday carry and both are built for water exposure. The decision is whether you want JBL's longer-running, more linkable rugged feature set or Bose's flatter body, microphone, and ecosystem conveniences.

JBL Flip 7

Buy this if / skip this if

Buy this if
  • You want the stronger outdoor spec sheet: JBL lists IP68 protection, one-meter drop resistance, and longer battery headroom than Bose.
  • You expect to link speakers for parties or larger patios; Auracast support makes the Flip 7 better for compatible JBL multi-speaker setups.
  • You care about audio controls and source flexibility; JBL lists app controls and USB-C lossless playback from compatible content and devices.
Skip this if
  • You want a flatter speaker body that slides into a narrow bag pocket instead of a cylindrical speaker shape.
  • You need a built-in microphone for calls from the speaker; JBL does not list one for the Flip 7 specs checked here.
  • You already use Bose speakers or soundbars and want Bose app and compatible Bose-product grouping features.
Bose SoundLink Flex 2nd Gen

Buy this if / skip this if

Buy this if
  • You prefer a flatter, rounded-rectangle speaker that can sit upright or lie down without taking the same bag shape as a cylinder.
  • You want a microphone on the speaker for quick calls or voice interactions without switching devices.
  • You use Bose gear already and value Bose app setup, SimpleSync-style compatible-product linking, and PositionIQ orientation handling.
Skip this if
  • You want the longest listed battery life in this comparison; Bose lists up to 12 hours versus JBL's longer listed range.
  • You want IP68 protection or a published one-meter drop claim from the speaker maker.
  • You plan to build a larger party chain around Auracast-compatible JBL speakers.
IP rating

IP68 gives JBL the stronger dust-and-water spec on paper. IP67 is still outdoor-friendly, but Bose also warns that the port needs to be fully dry before charging after water exposure.

Battery numbers

JBL's 14-hour listing and Playtime Boost ceiling are better for all-day use. Bose's 12-hour listing is still enough for most short outings, but leaves less cushion at higher volume.

Pairing ecosystem

Auracast matters if your friends or household own compatible JBL speakers. Bose is more appealing when you want Bose app features or compatible Bose speaker and soundbar linking.

Before you buy

Check these portable-speaker fit points first.

  • Think about where the speaker rides: a cylindrical speaker fits bottle pockets and tables differently than a flatter rounded-rectangle speaker.
  • Match the pairing ecosystem to what friends or family already own; JBL Auracast and Bose grouping features are not the same compatibility path.
  • Do not charge either speaker while wet. After beach, pool, or rain use, dry the port area before plugging in USB-C power.
  • Skip live price comparisons unless you are checking the final listing at checkout; color, bundle, and retailer availability can change quickly.

FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Which speaker is better for most people?

JBL Flip 7 is the stronger all-around rugged speaker because JBL lists longer play time, IP68 protection, Bluetooth 5.4, Auracast pairing, USB-C lossless audio support, and app-based audio controls.

Why would someone choose the Bose SoundLink Flex 2nd Gen?

Choose the Bose SoundLink Flex 2nd Gen if you prefer a flatter speaker body, a built-in microphone, Bose app and SimpleSync features, a nylon utility loop, and a speaker that Bose lists as floating when it gets wet.

Are both speakers waterproof?

Both are designed for wet conditions. JBL lists the Flip 7 with an IP68 rating, while Bose lists the SoundLink Flex 2nd Gen with IP67 water and dust resistance and notes that the USB-C port should be dry before charging.

Which speaker has longer battery life?

JBL lists up to 14 hours of music play time for Flip 7 and up to 16 hours with Playtime Boost. Bose lists up to 12 hours for the SoundLink Flex 2nd Gen.

Do either of these work as a speakerphone?

Bose lists a microphone on the SoundLink Flex 2nd Gen comparison table. JBL does not list a microphone for the Flip 7 in the official specs checked for this page.
