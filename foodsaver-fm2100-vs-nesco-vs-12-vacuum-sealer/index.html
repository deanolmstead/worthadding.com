Verdict

Which one should most people buy?

NESCO is the stronger buy when you seal different food textures, make freezer batches, or want more control over pressure and seal behavior. FoodSaver is the cleaner choice for shoppers who want a familiar, simpler countertop sealer with a long listed warranty and fewer mode decisions.

Best for most

NESCO VS-12

NESCO lists a 130-watt double-pump system, normal and gentle vacuum pressure, dry, moist, and double sealing, pulse vacuum, canister mode, seal-only mode, built-in roll storage and cutter, and bag material support up to 11.8 inches wide.

Buy the VS-12 unless simple FoodSaver-system use matters more than control.

The VS-12 wins because its extra controls solve real countertop problems. Gentle pressure helps with crush-prone foods, pulse gives you a manual way to stop before over-compressing a bag, and the double-seal option is useful when you are prepping moist or long-freezer items.

The FM2100 still makes sense for a simpler kitchen. FoodSaver lists built-in roll storage, a roll cutter, accessory hose compatibility, a removable dishwasher-safe drip tray, and a 5-year limited warranty. If you mostly seal dry pantry goods or routine freezer portions, that simpler control layout may be enough.

Buyer guide

Choose by prep style.

Both are countertop food vacuum sealers with roll storage and cutters, so the practical decision is control versus simplicity: pressure modes, seal modes, bag width, warranty expectations, and how often you seal mixed textures.

FoodSaver FM2100

Buy this if / skip this if

Buy this if
  • You want a straightforward FoodSaver-system countertop sealer with built-in roll storage and an included accessory hose.
  • You mostly seal dry pantry goods, simple freezer portions, or ordinary bags where one-touch simplicity is more important than pressure tuning.
  • You value FoodSaver's listed 5-year limited warranty and a shallower 9.2-inch published depth.
Skip this if
  • You frequently seal delicate foods and want a listed gentle pressure mode or pulse vacuum control.
  • You want dry, moist, and double seal modes on the unit itself.
  • You plan to use wider bag material and want an official 11.8-inch compatibility claim.
NESCO VS-12

Buy this if / skip this if

Buy this if
  • You batch prep freezer bags and want normal/gentle pressure plus pulse vacuum for better control over varied foods.
  • You seal moist foods often enough to want dry, moist, and double seal options built into the control panel.
  • You want canister mode, seal-only mode, a double-pump design, and bag material support up to 11.8 inches wide.
Skip this if
  • You want the simplest possible control layout and do not expect to use pulse, gentle pressure, or double seal modes.
  • Your counter or storage shelf is tight; NESCO lists a larger 17 x 15 x 9-inch body.
  • A longer listed limited warranty matters more to you than extra control modes.
Pressure control

Normal and gentle pressure are useful when you switch between sturdy freezer portions and crush-prone items. Without that choice, you rely more on timing and manual handling.

Seal modes

Dry, moist, and double seal modes matter when the bag edge may be harder to seal cleanly. The VS-12 gives you more built-in options for that workflow.

Footprint

The FoodSaver is shallower on paper, while the NESCO is larger and heavier. If the sealer lives in a cabinet, measure the shelf and the clearance for opening the lid.

Before you buy

Check these vacuum sealer fit points first.

  • Measure both counter width and storage depth; roll-storage sealers take more room when the lid is open.
  • Match bags and rolls to the machine width. NESCO lists support up to 11.8 inches wide; FoodSaver's checked page lists an included 11-inch roll.
  • For wet or sauced foods, expect more prep discipline. Chill, blot, or position contents so liquid does not interfere with the seal area.
  • Confirm accessory needs before checkout. Canisters, jar sealers, wine stoppers, and hose accessories are not interchangeable across every setup.

FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Which vacuum sealer is better for most home prep?

The NESCO VS-12 is the stronger pick for shoppers who batch prep, seal moist foods, or want more control because it adds normal and gentle pressure, dry, moist, and double seal modes, pulse vacuum, and canister mode. The FoodSaver FM2100 is better if you want a simpler FoodSaver-system unit with a longer listed limited warranty.

Does the NESCO VS-12 handle wider bags?

NESCO lists compatibility with bag material up to 11.8 inches wide. FoodSaver lists roll storage and a cutter, plus included quart bags and an 11-inch roll, but the checked FoodSaver page does not position the FM2100 as the wider-bag option.

Which one is better for moist foods?

Choose the NESCO VS-12 if moist-food control is a priority. NESCO lists dry, moist, and double sealing choices plus normal and gentle vacuum pressure. With either machine, avoid pulling free liquid into the seal area and follow the product manual for the food type you are sealing.

Is the FoodSaver FM2100 still worth buying?

Yes, if you want a simpler countertop sealer with built-in roll storage, a cutter, accessory hose support, a dishwasher-safe removable drip tray, and a 5-year limited warranty listed by FoodSaver. It gives up the VS-12's richer pressure and seal controls.
