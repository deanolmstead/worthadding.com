Before you buy

Check how the hand vac will live between cleanups.

  • Storage changes usage: a docked vacuum gets grabbed more often, but a bigger-bin unit may be more useful for car and stair jobs.
  • Small dust cups fill quickly: WV201's 0.1 qt cup is fine for crumbs, but not ideal for larger repeated debris passes.
  • Tools matter more than peak claims: crevice reach, brushes, and upholstery tools decide whether the vacuum fits your actual messes.
  • Neither is a floor-vac replacement: treat both as spot-cleaning tools for dry debris, furniture, car interiors, and quick pickups.

FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Which one is better for most households?

BLACK+DECKER CHV1410L is the safer default because its larger 605 ml dustbin, 11-minute listed runtime, rotating nozzle, extendable crevice tool, and flip-up brush cover more everyday cleanup jobs.

Why buy Shark WANDVAC WV201 instead?

Buy WV201 if you want a slimmer hand vac that stays on a countertop charging dock and comes with pet and duster-crevice tools for fast visible-access touchups.

Which one has the larger dust cup?

BLACK+DECKER CHV1410L. BLACK+DECKER lists a 605 ml dustbin, while Shark lists WV201 at 0.1 qt.

Are these good for car cleaning?

Both can handle small car debris, but CHV1410L is the better default for cars because the larger bin and extendable crevice tool are more forgiving around mats, seats, and tight gaps.

Can either replace a full-size vacuum?

No. These are spot-cleaning tools for dry debris, upholstery, stairs, car interiors, and quick crumbs. Use a stick, upright, or canister vacuum for full rooms.
