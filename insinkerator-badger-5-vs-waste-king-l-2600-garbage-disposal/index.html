Choose by install path, not just horsepower.

Both are 1/2 HP continuous-feed disposals. The real decision is whether you want Waste King's stronger spec-per-dollar setup or InSinkErator's familiar replacement path.

InSinkErator Badger 5

  • You want a known Badger-series model and are comfortable with galvanized grind components for everyday kitchen scraps.

Mount style

Mount style can matter more than horsepower when replacing a disposal. A model that fits the existing sink hardware can reduce install friction, even if another model has better raw specs.

Cord setup

Waste King's official data lists a pre-installed power cord. Badger 5 listings and variants can differ, so confirm whether your setup needs a plug-in cord or hardwire configuration before checkout.

Grind materials

Waste King lists stainless steel grind components, while InSinkErator's comparison chart lists galvanized steel for Badger 5. That is the clearest spec reason Waste King wins for new installs.

Before you buy

Check these under-sink fit points first.

  • Measure the vertical space under the sink, including drain line, trap, and stored items you still need to keep there.
  • Match the electrical setup. Some kitchens use a plug-in outlet; others are hardwired, and product variants may differ.
  • Confirm dishwasher drain connection needs before removing the old unit or ordering installation accessories.
  • Do not use horsepower alone as the deciding metric. Mount compatibility, cord setup, warranty, grind materials, and under-sink clearance all affect ownership.

FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Which garbage disposal is better for most fresh installs?

The Waste King L-2600 is the stronger default for many fresh installs because its official specs list stainless steel grind components, a 2600 RPM permanent magnet motor, EZ Mount, included power cord, and a 5-year limited warranty.

When should I choose the InSinkErator Badger 5 instead?

Choose the InSinkErator Badger 5 if you are replacing an existing InSinkErator-style setup and want the brand's familiar mounting ecosystem, Lift and Latch installation support, galvanized grind components, and 5-year in-home limited warranty.

Are both models 1/2 HP continuous-feed disposals?

Yes. The official InSinkErator and Waste King sources identify both models as 1/2 horsepower continuous-feed garbage disposals.

Do both models include a power cord?

Waste King's official L-2600 data lists a pre-installed power cord. InSinkErator sells Badger 5 configurations with and without a cord, so shoppers should match the retailer listing and local installation setup before buying.

What should I confirm before checkout?

Confirm the mount style, cord configuration, under-sink clearance, dishwasher drain needs, and exact model variant before checkout.
