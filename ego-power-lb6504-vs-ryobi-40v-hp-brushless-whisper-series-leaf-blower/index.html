EGO Power+ LB6504 vs Ryobi 40V HP Brushless Whisper Series

EGO Power+ LB6504 and Ryobi's 40V HP Brushless Whisper Series both promise strong cordless clearing power without gas, cords, or fumes. The real trade-off is raw output versus portability. The Ryobi is the better everyday buy unless weight or a single-tool EGO setup matters more.

Both blowers use brushless motors, a variable-speed trigger, and a dedicated turbo button for maximum output. The Ryobi 40V HP Brushless Whisper Series pulls ahead for most buyers because it moves more air (730 CFM versus 650 CFM) at a higher top speed (190 MPH versus 180 MPH), runs quieter at a rated 57 dB against EGO's 64 dB, and ships with two 4.0 Ah batteries plus a rapid charger instead of one, so you are not stuck waiting on a recharge mid-yard.

EGO Power+ LB6504 is still the right call if a lighter, more compact tool matters more than raw output. Its bare-tool weight of about 4.77 lbs beats the Ryobi's 6.4 lbs, which is noticeable during long one-handed sweeps, and its 56V battery slots into an existing EGO 56V tool lineup rather than starting a new 40V ecosystem. The trade-off is less airflow, a louder motor, and only one battery in the box.

Fit check

Choose by yard, not the brand on the housing.

The right pick depends less on brand loyalty and more on whether you value maximum airflow and runtime, or the lightest possible tool to carry for a long stretch of yard work.

Buy EGO Power+ LB6504 if

You already own EGO 56V tools and want to share one battery ecosystem, or you prioritize a lighter, more compact blower for long one-handed sweeps around a smaller yard or patio.

Skip it if you have a big yard with wet leaves or heavy debris -- 650 CFM and a single battery will run out of both power and runtime faster than the Ryobi kit.

Buy Ryobi 40V HP Brushless Whisper Series if

You want more airflow and top speed for heavier debris, a quieter motor for early mornings or close neighbors, and two batteries so you are not stuck recharging mid-job.

Skip it if the extra 1.6 lbs of bare-tool weight matters more to you than raw power, or you are already invested in EGO's 56V tool lineup.

FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Which blower moves more air?

The Ryobi 40V HP Brushless Whisper Series moves more air. Ryobi's own spec sheet lists 730 CFM and 190 MPH with the included speed tip, versus EGO's published 650 CFM and 180 MPH with its tapered nozzle attached for the LB6504. That gap shows up most on wet leaves and packed grass clippings.

Which blower is quieter?

The Ryobi is quieter. Ryobi rates the RY404100 at 57 dB as part of its Whisper Series line, while EGO's published spec for the LB6504 is 64 dB. That is a meaningful difference for anyone blowing leaves near neighbors or early in the morning.

How many batteries come with each blower?

The Ryobi kit (RY404100) ships with two 40V 4.0 Ah batteries and a rapid charger, giving roughly 2 hours of combined runtime per Ryobi's spec sheet. The EGO LB6504 ships with one 56V 5.0Ah battery and a 210W charger, rated for up to 90 minutes of runtime on its own.

Which blower is lighter to hold?

The EGO LB6504 is lighter as a bare tool at about 4.77 lbs versus the Ryobi's 6.4 lbs bare, per each brand's published specs. Once a battery is attached both land close to 9.7 lbs, but the EGO's lighter frame is easier to hold one-handed for long stretches.

Do both blowers have a turbo or boost mode?

Yes. Both use a variable-speed trigger plus a dedicated turbo button for maximum output, and the Ryobi additionally includes a cruise control feature that lets you lock in a speed without holding the trigger down, which the EGO LB6504 does not offer.

Are the battery systems interchangeable with other yard tools?

Not with each other. EGO's 56V ARC Lithium battery is compatible with EGO's own lineup of 100-plus tools, and Ryobi's 40V battery works across Ryobi's 40V outdoor power equipment line of 75-plus tools. Pick based on which battery ecosystem you already own or plan to build into, since the packs are not cross-compatible.
