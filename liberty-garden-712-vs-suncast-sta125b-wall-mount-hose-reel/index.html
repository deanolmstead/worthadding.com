Head-to-head wall-mount hose reel comparison

Liberty Garden 712 vs Suncast STA125B

These two wall-mounted reels solve the same daily problem: keep up to 125 feet of 5/8-inch hose organized instead of dragging it across the garden. The Liberty Garden 712 is a bronze steel reel with a rotating carriage and storage basket. The Suncast STA125B is a compact resin reel with a hose guide, included leader hose, and a removable reel. The useful decision is whether a fixed, directional steel reel or a simpler compact storage routine fits your wall and watering path.

Buy the Suncast STA125B unless the Liberty Garden 712’s rotating carriage is the feature your wall layout actually needs.

A hose reel earns its place in the routine between watering and putting the yard back in order. It should keep a hose controlled as it comes off the wall, wind it back without a messy pile, and avoid becoming another bulky item to solve at season’s end. Suncast lists the STA125B at 18.25 inches wide, 11.25 inches deep, and 21 inches high, with capacity for up to 125 feet of standard 5/8-inch vinyl hose. Its hose guide is explicitly there to keep hose lined up for even winding, and Suncast says the reel detaches for off-season storage.

Those details make a coherent default. The STA125B is a compact wall-mounted resin reel, needs no assembly according to Suncast, and includes a leader hose. A buyer can evaluate the size, use the guide each time the hose is rewound, and lift the reel off the mount when the season changes.

The Liberty Garden 712 is the specialized alternative. Its carriage rotates 360 degrees when lifted and locks in four positions, a real benefit when a hose regularly heads in different directions from one wall. Liberty also lists a basket, 12.5-pound weight, and 22 by 20.25 by 18.6-inch footprint. But it excludes water hose and mounting hardware. For a typical compact garden-wall installation, the Suncast’s guide, leader hose, removable reel, and smaller depth are more broadly useful.

Fit check

Choose by routine, not hose-reel snobbery.

Measure the wall, trace where the hose needs to travel, and decide whether seasonal removal matters. Those questions are more useful than calling one material universally better.

Buy the Suncast STA125B if

You want a compact wall reel to hold a standard 5/8-inch hose up to the maker’s listed 125-foot capacity, and you value a guided rewinding routine. The 11.25-inch published depth is a useful planning number along a narrow side yard or patio wall. The hose guide’s stated job is keeping the hose accurately lined up as it winds; that is exactly the kind of ordinary detail that reduces the chance of leaving a tangled coil after watering.

The Suncast is also the practical choice if you want to remove the reel for off-season storage rather than leave the full reel in place. Suncast says the unit requires no assembly, includes a leader hose, and is made from durable resin. Confirm the mounting surface, clearances, hose path, and instructions before installation, but for an uncomplicated garden faucet this is the straightforward format.

Skip it if the hose leaves the wall in different directions often enough that a rotating carriage is the reason you started comparing reels.

Buy the Liberty Garden 712 if

You have a fixed wall location but need flexibility in the hose departure angle. Liberty says the 712’s carriage can rotate 360 degrees when lifted, or secure in one of four positions when released. That makes sense where a corner, planting bed, driveway edge, or two separate watering zones pull the hose in different directions from the same faucet. Its basket is useful if a nozzle or small garden accessory belongs at the reel.

Its listed steel construction and bronze finish may also fit the appearance and feel you want on an exposed garden wall. The trade-off is planning: Liberty lists water hose and mounting hardware as not included, and the 18.6-inch depth needs more space than the compact Suncast body. Treat the listed 125-foot capacity and dimensions as the start of the installation check, not a promise for every hose or wall.

Skip it if a simple detachable resin reel, included leader hose, and a hose guide are more useful than directional rotation.

Fit-check callout: before ordering either one, measure the clear wall area plus the hose’s turning space. A reel can fit the wall yet still make a tight path awkward when the hose leaves at an angle.

FAQ

Questions before checkout.

Which wall-mount hose reel is the better pick for most people?

The Suncast STA125B is the better pick for most people because it holds up to 125 feet of standard 5/8-inch vinyl hose in a compact 18.25 by 11.25 by 21-inch resin wall reel, includes a leader hose, uses a hose guide for even winding, and detaches for off-season storage. Choose the Liberty Garden 712 when its steel construction, rotating carriage, four locking positions, and storage basket match a fixed garden-wall routine.

Do both reels hold the same amount of hose?

Yes, both makers describe the selected reels around a 125-foot capacity for 5/8-inch hose. Capacity is not a promise for every hose construction or winding pattern. Check the hose you plan to use and follow the manufacturer installation and use instructions.

What is the practical advantage of the Liberty Garden 712 rotating carriage?

Liberty Garden says the 712 carriage can rotate 360 degrees when its handle is lifted, then lock into one of four positions. That can be useful when the hose needs to leave the reel in several directions from one fixed wall location. It is a feature to choose for the yard layout, not a universal upgrade.

Which hose reel is easier to remove for winter storage?

Suncast specifically says the STA125B reel detaches from its wall mount for off-season storage. Liberty lists the 712 as a wall-mounted steel reel; compare the published dimensions and the mounting location before installing either model.

Are the supplied leader-hose details the same?

No. Suncast lists a leader hose as included with the STA125B. Liberty says water hose and mounting hardware are not included with the 712. Verify the current package contents from the seller before checkout because listings and bundles can vary.

Should dimensions and hose capacity be treated as an installation guarantee?

No. Manufacturer dimensions and capacities are planning information, not a guarantee for a particular wall, hose, or installation. Confirm the wall location, required mounting method, hose path, and clearances with the current manufacturer instructions before buying or mounting either reel.
